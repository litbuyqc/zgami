What is an Oopbuy spreadsheet?

An Oopbuy spreadsheet is a product-link list used to organize shopping research. Depending on its creator, it may include categories, prices, notes or pictures. Treat it as a discovery tool: inclusion does not verify quality, authenticity, availability or suitability for your destination.

03 / How to use an Oopbuy spreadsheet

Build a shortlist you can actually compare.

  1. STEP 01

    Choose one purpose

    Start with a specific need: daily shoes, a warm layer or a training outfit. Browse the matching category and save only relevant candidates.

  2. STEP 02

    Record the same details

    For each item, note the source link, size measurements, materials, selected variant and listed price. Mark missing information for follow-up.

  3. STEP 03

    Verify before ordering

    Reopen the source listing. Check the seller, current options, return terms, destination restrictions and full payable cost before proceeding.

What is an Oopbuy spreadsheet?

An Oopbuy spreadsheet is a product-link list used to organize shopping research. Its contents depend on its creator and may include categories, prices, notes or pictures. Inclusion does not verify product quality or authenticity.

Does this page provide a downloadable spreadsheet?

No. This page is an independent browsing guide with links to CAFANS categories. It does not host a spreadsheet download or verify whether linked products can be ordered through Oopbuy.

How should I compare clothing sizes?

Compare the listing’s garment measurements with a similar item you own. Check units, measurement methods and fit notes instead of relying only on S, M or L labels.

Are the brand illustrations verified product listings?

No. The Nike, Louis Vuitton, Gucci and Hermès graphics are original editorial illustrations. They do not show verified inventory, establish authenticity or imply brand affiliation.

What should I check before ordering?

Verify the exact item, seller, measurements, materials, current availability, return terms and destination restrictions. Compare the total payable cost, including applicable service fees, delivery, taxes and duties.
